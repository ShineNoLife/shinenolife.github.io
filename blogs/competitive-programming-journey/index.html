Collection of my past competitive programming journeys

I started my competitive programming journey when i was 13. Since then, I have participated in many competitions and events. Here is a collection of the interesting things I have done throughout that journey.

During 8th grade, I had no particular idea of what programming was. But since some of my online friends were programmers, I decided to try out the Informatics training class at my school. There I learned to write my first line of code in Pascal and learned to solve basic problems. I was fascinated by how these things worked, and I took a liking to solving problems that the teachers gave me.

Later on, I continued to spend my time learning and solving harder problems, eventually getting into the City's Olympiad Team and getting the First Prize at the Provincial Informatics Contest for secondary school students. During this period, I was very interested in solving problems. They gave me a sense of challenge and beauty, which I enjoyed.

Competitions and achievements during my high school years

After the fun 9th grade, I spent time preparing for exams and got admitted to the best high school in my province, studying in an Informatics Specialized Class. Interestingly, I got the highest marks in the entire province, even though at that point I was not particularly a studious person.

I got to participate in many summer camps aimed at specialized students. Participating in these camps was a memorable aspect of my high school years, since I got to meet like-minded people who also enjoyed competitive programming. Later on, these people would be a big inspiration for me to keep improving.

The Vietnamese National Olympiad in Informatics is the most prestigious competition for high-school students specialized in Informatics across all high schools of Vietnam. Achieving a prize in this Olympiad was one of my biggest goals, and I was very passionate about it at this point in time.

Around early 11th grade, I was chosen for the National Olympiad team representing my province. I spent quite a lot of my time training during this period, which increased my skills significantly, and then I received a Second Prize in the 2023 National Olympiad. This was a big motivator to me at the time. It motivated me to learn more interesting and complicated techniques for solving problems. Although I did not perform as well during 12th grade, I received another Second Prize. I was quite sad about my performance, but nonetheless, pursuing competitive programming helped me develop a love for solving problems.

Some other interesting achievements

For my academic achievements, I was given the prize "10 Outstanding Young Faces of Quang Binh Province" for the year 2023, which was the first time I got onto live television.

I was also awarded the Odon Vallet Scholarship for my academic achievements in 2023.

Because I feel Informatics is a very interesting but underappreciated subject, the members of the National Olympiad team and I set out to found MaskTech Informatics Club, which holds competitive programming contests with prizes annually, along with general activities in the field of Informatics.

During the training periods for the National Olympiad, I got to rank Master on Codeforces, which is a popular competitive programming website. Rank Master was about the top ~2% at the time.

ICPC 2025 Asia Pacific Championship

ICPC, or the International Collegiate Programming Contest, is the oldest, largest, and most prestigious global programming competition for college students. At its core, it fosters problem-solving skills along with teamwork. It is the next step from the National Olympiads in high school.

In my undergraduate year at university, my team and I participated in ICPC National Vietnam, then the ICPC Asia Regional, which was held in Hanoi. We performed pretty well at the Regional contest, earning a top 10 place among all Vietnamese teams. We were then selected to participate in the ICPC 2025 Asia Pacific Championship, which had a total of 65 teams from universities across the Asia Pacific area. That was the first year my university was able to participate in the championship, and not only that, there were three teams going.

The 2025 ICPC Asia Pacific Championship was held in Singapore, hosted by the National University of Singapore. This was the first time I traveled overseas, and I was so excited for this event. During the Championship, I got to talk and socialize with people. There were even some quant companies giving out merch and hosting discussions. I also got to experience food from many cultures and beautiful places in Singapore. I particularly liked the trees in Singapore. You can see trees everywhere you go, which contributed to the country's refreshing atmosphere.
